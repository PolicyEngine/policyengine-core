Auto-carry-over now prefers, for a variable with a `set_input` helper, periods at the variable's definition unit, and uses other units only when it has nothing there. Since 3.24.0 it took the latest-starting period of any unit, so it could pick the twelfth of a yearly flow that `calculate_divide` caches at a month: a yearly input known only for one year came out at a twelfth of its value in later years once any month after January had been requested. In policyengine-us this set every person's age to a twelfth of its value in the second year of a multi-year simulation. Variables without a helper (DAY variables, `set_input = None`) are unchanged.
