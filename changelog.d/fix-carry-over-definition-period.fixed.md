Auto-carry-over now prefers values stored at the variable's own definition period, and uses a period in another unit only when there is none at its own unit. Since 3.24.0 it took the latest-starting period of any unit, so it could pick the twelfth of a yearly flow that `calculate_divide` caches at a month: a yearly input known only for one year came out at a twelfth of its value in later years once any month after January had been requested. In policyengine-us this set every person's age to a twelfth of its value in the second year of a multi-year simulation. Inputs that exist only at another unit still carry over.
