Auto-carry-over no longer carries a value core derived by rescaling another period's value: the twelfth of a yearly flow that `calculate_divide` caches at a month, or a sum over several sub-periods that `calculate_add` caches at the larger period. Such caches are now marked derived (`Holder.put_in_cache(..., derived=True)`) until the period is written again. Since 3.24.0 carry-over could pick a month's twelfth for a later year, so a yearly input known only for one year came out at a twelfth of its value in later years once any month after January had been requested; in policyengine-us this set every person's age to a twelfth of its value in the second year of a multi-year simulation. Inputs at any unit still carry over.
