Auto-carry-over now reads only values stored at the variable's own definition period. Since 3.24.0 it could pick a month value cached for a yearly variable (a twelfth of the year's value), so a yearly input known only for one year came out at a twelfth of its value in later years once any month after January had been requested; in policyengine-us this set every person's age to a twelfth of its value in the second year of a multi-year simulation.
