Auto-carry-over no longer carries a cache derived in another unit than the variable's definition period. Since 3.24.0 it could pick the twelfth of a yearly flow cached at a month by `calculate_divide` (or a monthly flow's year sum cached by `calculate_add`), so a yearly input known only for one year came out at a twelfth of its value in later years once any month after January had been requested. In policyengine-us this set every person's age to a twelfth of its value in the second year of a multi-year simulation. Inputs stored in another unit, by variables without a `set_input` helper, still carry over.
