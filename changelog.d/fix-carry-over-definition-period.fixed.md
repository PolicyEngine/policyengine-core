Auto-carry-over no longer carries a value held at a unit other than the variable's definition period when the variable has a `set_input` helper. A helper stores every input at the definition period, so such values are caches: the twelfth of a yearly flow that `calculate_divide` caches at a month, or a sum that `calculate_add` caches at a larger period. Since 3.24.0 carry-over took the latest-starting period of any unit, so a yearly input known only for one year came out at a twelfth of its value in later years once any month after January had been requested; in policyengine-us this set every person's age to a twelfth of its value in the second year of a multi-year simulation. Variables without a helper (DAY variables, and any declaring `set_input = None`) are unchanged.
