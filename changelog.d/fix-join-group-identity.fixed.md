Persons joined to groups from a dataset now land in the group their ID names even when a group with no members sorts before theirs, and IDs of different dtypes match by value; an ID that names no declared group, or a repeated group ID, now raises instead of silently picking a group. A flat-file dataset with a `household_id`-style column no longer declares the previous entity's IDs as its groups, and its default roles cover every person.
