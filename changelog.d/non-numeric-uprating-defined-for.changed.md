`uprating` on an Enum, `str` or date variable, and a `defined_for` that names one, are now rejected with a `ValueError` when the variables are registered (or when `uprating` is assigned), so a tax-benefit system containing either no longer loads; before, it loaded and raised `TypeError` when the variable was uprated or calculated, or, for a group variable `defined_for` a person Enum, masked on the sum of its members' Enum indices. `defined_for` must name a `bool`, `int` or `float` variable.
