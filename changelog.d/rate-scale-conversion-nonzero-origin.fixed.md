`MarginalRateTaxScale.to_average` now keeps a first threshold other than 0, so the average scale levies the marginal tax there and `to_marginal` gives the scale back; it raises `ValueError` instead of returning a scale that taxes too little for one bracket with a nonzero rate away from 0 or for tax at a threshold of 0, and gives one bracket with surplus rates the same result as without them.
