A simulation's temporary on-disk storage folder (`openfisca_*`) is now removed once the simulation and every clone, branch or disk storage that reads files in it are garbage-collected, or at interpreter exit, instead of being left behind, except the subfolders of disk storages that preserve theirs (`preserve_storage_dir`). Clones and forked processes now store new on-disk values in folders of their own, and pickled, copied or forked disk storages write new files rather than over ones another storage reads, so none of them writes over or removes another's files; nor does a disk storage remove the folder containing its own, such as a folder the caller chose as `_data_storage_dir`.
